# 数学-5/11-积分类

### T1-赋值法求有理函数积分

#### 题意：

求不定积分：

$$
I=\int\frac{2x^{2}+2x+13}{(x-2)(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x
$$
</details>
<details> <summary>题解</summary>


将积分分解为部分分式之和：

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2x^2 + 2x + 13}{(x-2)(x^2+1)^2} = \frac{a}{x-2} + \frac{bx+c}{x^2+1} + \frac{dx+e}{(x^2+1)^2}, \end{aligned}
$$

有：

$$
2x^{2}+2x+13=a\left(x^{2}+1\right)^{2}+\left(bx+c\right)\left(x-2\right)\left(x^{2}+1\right)+\left(dx+e\right)\left(x-2\right)
$$

采用赋值法确定系数

令$x = 2$，得 $a = 1$

令$x = i$ ，得$11+2i=(di+e)(i-2)=-d-2e+(e-2d)i$

则：

$$
\begin{aligned} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} -d-2e=11, \\ e-2d=2, \end{array} \right. \end{aligned}
$$

故 $d = -3,e = -4$

令$x = 0$，得$c = -2$

令$x = 1$，得 $b = -1$

故：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int \left[ \frac{1}{x-2} + \frac{-x-2}{x^2+1} + \frac{-3x-4}{(x^2+1)^2} \right] \mathrm{d}x \\
  &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x-2} - \int \frac{x+2}{x^2+1} \mathrm{d}x - \int \frac{3x+4}{(x^2+1)^2} \mathrm{d}x \\
  &= \ln |x-2| - \int \frac{x}{x^2+1} \mathrm{d}x - 2 \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2+1} - 3 \int \frac{x}{(x^2+1)^2} \mathrm{d}x - 4 \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x^2+1)^2} \\
  &= \ln |x-2| - \frac{1}{2} \ln (x^2+1) - 2 \arctan x + \frac{3}{2(x^2+1)} - \frac{2x}{x^2+1} - 2 \arctan x + C \\
  &= \ln |x-2| - \frac{1}{2} \ln (x^2+1) - \frac{4x-3}{2(x^2+1)} - 4 \arctan x + C.
\end{aligned}
$$

### T2-换元法处理有理函数积分

#### 题意：

求不定积分：

$$
I=\int\frac{\mathrm{~d}x}{x^{2}\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{2}}
$$

</details>
<details> <summary>题解</summary>

显然的做法是采用部分分式求解，分解后要求解6个待定系数，运算量非常大，采用换元积分可以简化很多

令 $x = tant$，则

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int \frac{\sec^2 t dt}{\tan^2 t \sec^4 t} = \int \frac{dt}{\tan^2 t \sec^2 t} = \int \frac{\sec^2 t - \tan^2 t}{\tan^2 t \sec^2 t} dt \\
  &= \int \frac{dt}{\tan^2 t} - \int \frac{dt}{\sec^2 t} \\
  &= \int (\csc^2 t - 1) dt - \int \cos^2 t dt \\
  &= -\cot t - t - \frac{t}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \sin 2t + C \\
  &= -\frac{1}{x} - \frac{3}{2} \arctan x - \frac{x}{2(1+x^2)} + C.
\end{aligned}
$$

### T3-换元法处理有理函数积分

#### 题意：

$$
\begin{aligned} \ I=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{(\sin x+2 \sec x)^{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>
<details> <summary>题解</summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int \frac{\sec^2 x \, dx}{\sec^2 x (\sin x + 2 \sec x)^2} \\
  &= \int \frac{d(\tan x)}{(2 \tan^2 x + \tan x + 2)^2} \\
  &\underset{t = \tan x}{=} \int \frac{dt}{(2t^2 + t + 2)^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

考虑不定积分$I_{1}=\int\frac{\mathrm{~d}t}{2t^{2}+t+2}$,凑微分：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{dt}{\left(t + \frac{1}{4}\right)^2 + \frac{15}{16}} \\
    &= \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}} \arctan \frac{t + \frac{1}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}} + C \\
    &= \frac{2}{\sqrt{15}} \arctan \frac{4t + 1}{\sqrt{15}} + C
\end{aligned}
$$

另一方面，利用分部积分：

$$
\begin{aligned}\\I_{1} &= \frac{t}{2t^{2} + t + 2} + \int \frac{t(4t + 1)dt}{(2t^{2} + t + 2)^{2}} \\\\&= \frac{t}{2t^{2} + t + 2} + \int \frac{2(2t^{2} + t + 2) - \left(t + \frac{1}{4}\right) - \frac{15}{4}}{(2t^{2} + t + 2)^{2}} dt \\\\&= \frac{t}{2t^{2} + t + 2} + 2 \int \frac{dt}{2t^{2} + t + 2} - \frac{1}{4} \int \frac{d(2t^{2} + t + 2)}{(2t^{2} + t + 2)^{2}} - \frac{15}{4} \int \frac{dt}{(2t^{2} + t + 2)^{2}} \\\\&= \frac{t}{2t^{2} + t + 2} + 2I_{1} + \frac{1}{4(2t^{2} + t + 2)} - \frac{15}{4}I,\\\end{aligned}
$$

则：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \frac{4t+1}{15(2t^2+t+2)} + \frac{4}{15} I_1 \\
  &= \frac{4t+1}{15(2t^2+t+2)} + \frac{8}{15\sqrt{15}} \arctan \frac{4t+1}{\sqrt{15}} + C_1 \\
  &= \frac{4\tan x + 1}{15(2\tan^2 x + \tan x + 2)} + \frac{8}{15\sqrt{15}} \arctan \frac{4\tan x + 1}{\sqrt{15}} + C_1.
\end{aligned}
$$
