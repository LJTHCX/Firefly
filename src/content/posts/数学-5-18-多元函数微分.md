<summary>二元函数的极限</summary>

<details>
<summary>T1-证明二重极限存在</summary>

**题意**

证明：

$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y \to 0}} \frac{x^3 + y^3}{x^2 + y^2} = 0
$$

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

当 $(x,y) \neq (0,0)$ 时，
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left|\frac{x^3 + y^3}{x^2 + y^2}\right| &\le \frac{x^2}{x^2 + y^2} |x| + \frac{y^2}{x^2 + y^2} |y| \\
&\le |x| + |y|
\end{aligned}
$$
对于任意 $\varepsilon > 0$，取 $\delta = \frac{\varepsilon}{2}$，则当 $|x| < \delta, |y| < \delta, (x,y) \neq (0,0)$ 时，
$$
\left|\frac{x^{3}+y^{3}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right| \leqslant |x|+|y| < \frac{\varepsilon}{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2} = \varepsilon.
$$
则：
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y \to 0}} \frac{x^{3} + y^{3}}{x^{2} + y^{2}} = 0
$$

</details>

<details>
<summary>关于 $\varepsilon-\delta$（GemPro生成）</summary>

二元函数极限的严格数学定义（即 $\varepsilon-\delta$ 语言定义）是微积分中的核心概念。它将“无限靠近”这种直观感觉，转化为了严密的数学逻辑。

**二元函数极限的 $\varepsilon-\delta$ 定义**

设函数 $z = f(x,y)$ 在点 $P_0(x_0, y_0)$ 的某个**去心邻域**内有定义。如果存在一个常数 $A$，满足以下条件：

对于**任意**给定的正数 $\varepsilon > 0$（无论它多么小），**总存在**一个正数 $\delta > 0$，使得当点 $P(x,y)$ 与点 $P_0(x_0, y_0)$ 的距离满足：
$$
0 < \sqrt{(x-x_0)^2 + (y-y_0)^2} < \delta
$$
时，对应的函数值 $f(x,y)$ 必定满足不等式：
$$
|f(x,y) - A| < \varepsilon
$$
那么，常数 $A$ 就叫做函数 $f(x,y)$ 当 $(x,y) \to (x_0, y_0)$ 时的极限，记作：
$$
\lim_{(x,y) \to (x_0,y_0)} f(x,y) = A
$$

**核心要素拆解与几何意义**

1. **误差容忍度 $\varepsilon$**：代表在 $z$ 轴（函数值）上，我们允许函数值 $f(x,y)$ 偏离目标极限值 $A$ 的最大误差范围。$|f(x,y) - A| < \varepsilon$ 意味着函数值必须落在区间 $(A-\varepsilon, A+\varepsilon)$ 内部。

2. **控制范围 $\delta$**：代表在 $xy$ 平面（自变量）上，点 $P(x,y)$ 距离目标点 $P_0(x_0, y_0)$ 的最大允许距离。这个距离是一个以 $(x_0, y_0)$ 为圆心、$\delta$ 为半径的圆（去掉圆心）。

3. **“去心”的意义 $0 < \dots$**：公式里的 $0 < \sqrt{(x-x_0)^2 + (y-y_0)^2}$ 意味着点 $(x,y)$ 可以无限靠近 $(x_0, y_0)$，但**绝不能等于**$(x_0, y_0)$。这是因为极限只关心函数在某点**附近**的变化趋势，而不在乎函数在该点本身是否有定义。

4. **逻辑顺序：先有 $\varepsilon$，后找 $\delta$**：这是一个“你出招，我拆招”的过程：不论别人给出一个多么苛刻（多么小）的 $\varepsilon$，你都必须能计算/找到一个相应的 $\delta$ 来“锁住”自变量的范围，保证函数值不超标。

<summary>T2-证明二重极限不存在</summary>

**题意**

证明：
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \rightarrow 0 \\ y \rightarrow 0}} \frac{x y}{\sqrt{x+y+1}-1} \text{ 不存在.}
$$

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

$$
\frac{x y}{\sqrt{x+y+1}-1} = \frac{x y}{x+y}(\sqrt{x+y+1}+1)
$$
而 $\lim_{\substack{x \rightarrow 0 \\ y \rightarrow 0}}(\sqrt{x+y+1}+1)=2$ 存在，故只需证明 $\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y \to 0}} \frac{xy}{x + y}$ 不存在即可。

当 $(x,y)$ 沿着 $y = kx^2 - x(k \neq 0)$ 趋向于 $(0,0)$ 时，
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y \to 0}} \frac{xy}{x + y} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{kx - 1}{k} = -\frac{1}{k}
$$
此结果随之不同的 $k$ 而变化，故此极限不存在，原极限不存在。

</details>

</details>

<summary>T3-迫敛定理求二重极限</summary>

**题意**

求极限：
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to \infty \\ y \to \infty}} \frac{x^2 + xy + y^2}{x^4 + y^4} \left( \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2} \right)^{x^2}
$$

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

由均值不等式可得：
$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &\le \left|\frac{x^{2}+xy+y^{2}}{x^{4}+y^{4}}\left(\frac{xy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right)^{x^{2}}\right| \\
  &\le \frac{\frac{3}{2}(x^{2}+y^{2})}{2x^{2}y^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{x^{2}} \\
  &\le \left(\frac{1}{x^{2}}+\frac{1}{y^{2}}\right)\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{x^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
而：
$$
\lim_{x \to \infty} \left( \frac{1}{x^2} + \frac{1}{y^2} \right) = 0, \quad \lim_{x \to \infty} \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{x^2} = 0
$$
故：
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to \infty \\ y \to \infty}} \frac{x^2 + xy + y^2}{x^4 + y^4} \left( \frac{xy}{x+y} \right)^{x^2} = 0.
$$

</details>

</details>

<summary>T4-极坐标变换求二重极限</summary>

**题意**

求极限：
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to \infty \\ y \to \infty}} \frac{(x^2 + y^2) \sin(x^4 + y^4)}{x^4 + y^4}
$$

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

令 $x = \rho \cos \theta, y = \rho \sin \theta$，则：
$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &\leqslant \frac{(x^2 + y^2) \sin(x^4 + y^4)}{x^4 + y^4} \\
  &\leqslant \frac{x^2 + y^2}{x^4 + y^4} \\
  &= \frac{1}{\rho^2 \left(1 - \frac{1}{2} \sin^2 2\theta\right)} \\
  &\leqslant \frac{2}{\rho^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
而当 $x \to \infty, y \to \infty$ 时，$\rho \to \infty$，故
$$
\lim_{\substack{x \to \infty \\ y \to \infty}} \frac{(x^2 + y^2) \sin(x^4 + y^4)}{x^4 + y^4} = 0.
$$

<summary>二元函数连续性、可导性和可微性</summary>

关于二元函数的连续性、可导性（即偏导数存在）与可微性之间的关系可总结如下：

(1) 二元函数 $f(x,y)$ 可微则必连续，反之不然。

(2) 二元函数 $f(x,y)$ 可导不能推出 $f(x,y)$ 连续；若 $f(x,y)$ 的偏导数存在且有界，则 $f(x,y)$ 必连续。

(3) 二元函数 $f(x,y)$ 可微则必可导，但可导不一定可微；若偏导数连续（或其中一个连续，另一个存在）则一定可微；偏导数不连续也有可能可微。

<details>
<summary>T1-二元函数可导推不出连续</summary>

**题意**

设函数：
$$
f(x,y) = \begin{cases} 1, & xy \neq 0, \\ 0, & xy = 0. \end{cases}
$$
求 $f_x(0,0), f_y(0,0)$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

由 $f(x,0) = f(0,y) = f(0,0) = 0$，得：
$$
f_x(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(0 + \Delta x, 0) - f(0,0)}{\Delta x} = 0, \quad
f_y(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(0, 0 + \Delta y) - f(0,0)}{\Delta y} = 0.
$$
当 $x \to 0, y \to 0$ 时，显然二元函数的极限不存在，故 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(0,0)$ 处不连续。

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>T2-二元函数可导不一定可微</summary>

**题意**

证明：
$$
f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \frac{xy}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}, & (x,y) \neq (0,0), \\ 0, & (x,y) = (0,0) \end{cases}
$$
在点 $(0,0)$ 处存在偏导数但不可微。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

由：
$$
f_x(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(0 + \Delta x, 0) - f(0,0)}{\Delta x} = 0, \quad
f_y(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(0, 0 + \Delta y) - f(0,0)}{\Delta y} = 0,
$$
知 $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处两个偏导数均存在。

若 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(0,0)$ 处可微，则：
$$
\mathrm{d}f = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \mathrm{d}x + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \mathrm{d}y = 0, \quad \Delta f = \mathrm{d}f + o(\rho) = o(\rho)
$$
其中 $\rho = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$，则：
$$
o(\rho) = \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}
$$
故：
$$
\frac{o(\rho)}{\rho} = \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} \to 0 \quad (\Delta x \to 0, \Delta y \to 0).
$$
但这与二重极限 $\lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$ 不存在相矛盾，故 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(0,0)$ 处不可微。

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>T3-二元函数偏导不连续也可微</summary>

**题意**

已知：
$$
f(x,y) = \begin{cases} (x^2 + y^2) \sin \frac{1}{x^2 + y^2}, & x^2 + y^2 \neq 0, \\ 0, & x^2 + y^2 = 0. \end{cases}
$$
证明 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(0,0)$ 处偏导不连续，但可微。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

$$
f_x(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \Delta x \cdot \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2} = 0, \quad
f_y(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \Delta y \cdot \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta y)^2} = 0,
$$
故 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(0,0)$ 处偏导数存在，则：
$$
f_x(x,y) = \begin{cases} 2x \sin \frac{1}{x^2+y^2} - \frac{2x}{x^2+y^2} \cos \frac{1}{x^2+y^2}, & x^2 + y^2 \neq 0, \\ 0, & x^2 + y^2 = 0, \end{cases}
$$
$$
f_y(x,y) = \begin{cases} 2y \sin \frac{1}{x^2+y^2} - \frac{2y}{x^2+y^2} \cos \frac{1}{x^2+y^2}, & x^2 + y^2 \neq 0, \\ 0, & x^2 + y^2 = 0. \end{cases}
$$
因 $\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y = x}} f_x(x,y)$ 和 $\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y = x}} f_y(x,y)$ 都不存在，故 $f(x,y)$ 的两个偏导数在 $(0,0)$ 处均不连续。

由 $\Delta f = \left[(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2\right] \cdot \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$，$f_x(0,0) = f_y(0,0) = 0$，知在点 $(0,0)$ 处，当 $\rho = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} \to 0$ 时：
$$
\frac{\Delta f - \left[f_x(0,0) \cdot \Delta x + f_y(0,0) \cdot \Delta y\right]}{\rho} = \rho \sin \frac{1}{\rho^2} \to 0,
$$
故 $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处可微。

</details>

</details>

</details>

<summary>求复合函数的偏导数</summary>

区分中间变量与自变量，对某个自变量求偏导数时，须经过所有的中间变量归结到该自变量。

<details>
<summary>T1</summary>

**题意**

设 $z = f(x^2 - y^2, \cos xy)$，$x - r \cos \theta = 0, y - r \sin \theta = 0$。求 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}, \frac{\partial z}{\partial \theta}$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

设 $u = x^2 - y^2, v = \cos xy$，$f(u,v)$ 经过中间变量 $u,v$ 而后中间变量 $x,y$ 变成自变量 $r,\theta$ 的函数，则：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial z}{\partial r} &= \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \cdot \frac{\partial y}{\partial r} \\
&= \left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \right) \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} + \left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \right) \frac{\partial y}{\partial r} \\
&= 2 \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} (x \cos \theta - y \sin \theta) - \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \sin xy (y \cos \theta + x \sin \theta), \\
\frac{\partial z}{\partial \theta} &= \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \cdot \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \\
&= \left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \right) \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} + \left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \right) \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \\
&= -2r \frac{\partial z}{\partial u}(x \sin \theta + y \cos \theta) + r \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \sin xy(y \sin \theta - x \cos \theta).
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

</details>

<summary>T2</summary>

**题意**

设函数 $f(x,y)$ 具有连续偏导数，且对任意 $x,y$，满足
$$
\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)^2 = 4.
$$
又设 $g(u,v) = f\left(uv, \frac{1}{2}(u^2 - v^2)\right)$，且对任意 $u,v$，满足
$$
a\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial u}\right)^2 - b\left(\frac{\partial g}{\partial v}\right)^2 = u^2 + v^2,
$$
求常数 $a,b$ 的值。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

记 $x = uv, y = \frac{1}{2}(u^2 - v^2)$，则 $g(u,v)$ 是以 $x,y$ 为中间变量，以 $u,v$ 为自变量的复合函数：
$$
\frac{\partial g}{\partial u} = v \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} + u \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \quad
\frac{\partial g}{\partial v} = u \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} - v \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}.
$$
带入：
$$
a \left( \frac{\partial g}{\partial u} \right)^2 - b \left( \frac{\partial g}{\partial v} \right)^2 = u^2 + v^2
$$
整理：
$$
(av^2 - bu^2)\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)^2 + 2(a+b)uv\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} + (au^2 - bv^2)\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)^2 = u^2 + v^2
$$
带入 $\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)^2 = 4 - \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)^2$，整理：
$$
(a+b)(v^2-u^2)\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)^2 + 2(a+b)uv\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = (1-4a)u^2 + (1+4b)v^2.
$$
则有：
$$
a+b = 0,\quad 1-4a = 0,\quad 1+4b = 0.
$$
解得 $a = 1/4, b = -1/4$。

</details>

</details>

<summary>求隐函数的偏导数</summary>

(1) 由一个方程确定的一个隐函数

直接法：把 $x,y$ 看成独立变量，$z$ 看成 $x,y$ 的函数，方程两边对 $x,y$ 求导，联立解方程。

公式法：$x,y,z$ 均看作独立变量：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F_x}{F_z}, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{F_y}{F_z} \quad (F_z \neq 0).
$$

全微分法：基于全微分形式不变性原理。

<details>
<summary>T1</summary>

**题意**

设 $x \cos y + y \cos z + z \cos x = 1$，求 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

**(1) 直接法**

将方程两端分别对 $x,y$ 求导，得：
$$
\cos y - (y \sin z) \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - z \sin x + \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \cos x = 0
$$
$$
-x \sin y + \cos z - (y \sin z) \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \cos x = 0
$$
联立解得：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\cos y - z \sin x}{y \sin z - \cos x}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{\cos z - x \sin y}{y \sin z - \cos x}
$$

**(2) 公式法**

令 $F(x,y,z) = x \cos y + y \cos z + z \cos x - 1$，则：
$$
F_x = \cos y - z \sin x, \quad F_y = \cos z - x \sin y, \quad F_z = \cos x - y \sin z
$$
故：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F_x}{F_z} = \frac{\cos y - z \sin x}{y \sin z - \cos x}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{F_y}{F_z} = \frac{\cos z - x \sin y}{y \sin z - \cos x}
$$

**(3) 全微分法**

令 $F(x,y,z) = x \cos y + y \cos z + z \cos x - 1$，令 $F(x,y,z) = 0$，则：
$$
\mathrm{d}F = (\cos y - z \sin x)\mathrm{d}x + (\cos z - x \sin y)\mathrm{d}y + (\cos x - y \sin z)\mathrm{d}z = 0
$$
即：
$$
\mathrm{d}z = \frac{\cos y - z \sin x}{y \sin z - \cos x} \cdot \mathrm{d}x + \frac{\cos z - x \sin y}{y \sin z - \cos x} \cdot \mathrm{d}y
$$
故：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\cos y - z \sin x}{y \sin z - \cos x}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{\cos z - x \sin y}{y \sin z - \cos x}
$$

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>T2</summary>

**题意**

设 $x = e^u \cos v, y = e^u \sin v, z = uv$。试求 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ 和 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

**(1) 将 $u$ 和 $v$ 看成中间变量，$x$ 和 $y$ 看成自变量**

$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = v \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + u \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = v \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + u \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}
$$
易知函数显化：
$$
u = \frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2 + y^2), \quad v = \arctan\frac{y}{x}
$$
则：
$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2} = \frac{\cos v}{e^u}, \quad
\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{x^2 + y^2} = \frac{\sin v}{e^u}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = -\frac{y}{x^2 + y^2} = -\frac{\sin v}{e^u}, \quad
\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2} = \frac{\cos v}{e^u}
$$
故：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = e^{-u}(v \cos v - u \sin v), \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = e^{-u}(u \cos v + v \sin v)
$$

**(2) 将 $x$ 和 $y$ 看成中间变量，$u$ 和 $v$ 看成自变量**

$$
v = e^u (\cos v) \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + e^u (\sin v) \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}, \quad
u = -e^u (\sin v) \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + e^u (\cos v) \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}
$$
联立解得：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = e^{-u}(v \cos v - u \sin v), \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = e^{-u}(u \cos v + v \sin v)
$$

**(3) 采用全微分**

对给定的方程组求全微分，得：
$$
\mathrm{d}x = e^u (\cos v) \mathrm{d}u - e^u (\sin v) \mathrm{d}v, \quad
\mathrm{d}y = e^u (\sin v) \mathrm{d}u + e^u (\cos v) \mathrm{d}v, \quad
\mathrm{d}z = v \mathrm{d}u + u \mathrm{d}v
$$
联立前两式得：
$$
\mathrm{d}u = e^{-u}(\cos v \mathrm{d}x + \sin v \mathrm{d}y), \quad
\mathrm{d}v = e^{-u}(-\sin v \mathrm{d}x + \cos v \mathrm{d}y)
$$
带入 $\mathrm{d}z = v \mathrm{d}u + u \mathrm{d}v$，得：
$$
\mathrm{d}z = e^{-u}(v \cos v - u \sin v) \mathrm{d}x + e^{-u}(v \sin v + u \cos v) \mathrm{d}y
$$
故：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = e^{-u}(v \cos v - u \sin v), \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = e^{-u}(v \sin v + u \cos v)
$$

</details>

<summary>求高阶偏导数</summary>

<details>
<summary>T1-求复合函数的高阶偏导数</summary>

**题意**

设 $z = z(x,y)$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，满足方程
$$
3 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} - 2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = 0,
$$
已知变换
$$
\begin{cases} u = x + ay \\ v = x - 3y \end{cases}
$$
可将该方程化为 $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u \partial v} = 0$，求常数 $a$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

易知：
$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = a \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} - 3 \frac{\partial z}{\partial v}
$$
则：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + 2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u \partial v} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} = a \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + (a - 3) \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u \partial v} - 3 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = a^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} - 6a \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u \partial v} + 9 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
$$
代入原方程：
$$
(3 - 2a - a^2) \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + 4(a + 3) \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u \partial v} = 0
$$
故：
$$
3 - 2a - a^2 = 0, \quad a + 3 \neq 0
$$
所以 $a = 1$。

</details>

</details>

<summary>T2-简化表达式</summary>

**题意**

设 $x = e^{u+v}, y = e^{u-v}$，满足：
$$
x^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + y^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} + x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0
$$
证明：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} = 0
$$

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial u} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} e^{u+v} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} e^{u-v}, \quad
\frac{\partial z}{\partial v} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} e^{u+v} - \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} e^{u-v}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} = x^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + 2xy \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} + y^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} + x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} = x^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} - 2xy \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} + y^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} + x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}
$$
则：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} = 2 \left( x^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + y^2 \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} + x \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right) = 0
$$

<summary>T3-求隐函数的高阶偏导数</summary>

**题意**

设 $u = u(x,y)$ 是由方程 $u + e^u = xy$ 所确定的二元函数。求 $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y}$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

对方程两端求微分：
$$
\mathrm{d}u = \frac{y}{1 + e^u} \mathrm{d}x + \frac{x}{1 + e^u} \mathrm{d}y
$$
则：
$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{y}{1 + e^u}, \quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = \frac{x}{1 + e^u}
$$
故：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \right) = \frac{1 + e^u - y e^u \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}}{(1 + e^u)^2} = \frac{1}{1 + e^u} - \frac{x y e^u}{(1 + e^u)^3}
$$

<summary>T4-求隐函数的高阶偏导数</summary>

**题意**

$z = z(x,y)$ 是由方程 $f(2x, y, z - x) = 0$ 所确定的二元函数，其中函数 $f$ 具有二阶连续偏导数。求 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}$。

<details>
<summary>题解</summary>

令 $u = 2x, v = y, w = z - x$，则 $f(u,v,w) = 0$，两边对 $x$ 求偏导数，得：
$$
2f_u + f_w \left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - 1 \right) = 0, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = 1 - 2 \frac{f_u}{f_w}
$$
易知：
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(f_u) = 2f_{uu} + f_{uw}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - 1\right) = 2f_{uu} - 2f_{uw}\frac{f_u}{f_w}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(f_w) = 2f_{uw} + f_{ww}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - 1\right) = 2f_{uw} - 2f_{ww}\frac{f_u}{f_w}
$$
则：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = -\frac{2}{(f_w)^2} \left[ f_w \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(f_u) - f_u \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(f_w) \right] = -\frac{4}{(f_w)^3} \left[ f_{uu}(f_w)^2 - 2f_u f_w f_{uw} + f_{ww}(f_u)^2 \right]
$$

</details>
