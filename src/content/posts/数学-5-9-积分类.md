### <details><summary>T1-分部积分</summary>

#### <details><summary>题意：</summary>

$$
I=\int\frac{x^{2}(x\sec^{2}x+\tan x)}{(x\tan x+1)^{2}}dx
$$

</details>
<details> <summary><b>题解</b></summary>

注意到 ~~（注意力惊人）~~：

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} x}(x \tan x+1)=x \sec ^{2} x+\tan x \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(x \sin x + \cos x) = x \cos x;\end{aligned}
$$

那么~~显然~~：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int x^2 \, \mathrm{d}\left(-\frac{1}{x \tan x + 1}\right) \\
  &= -\frac{x^2}{x \tan x + 1} + \int \frac{2x}{x \tan x + 1} \, \mathrm{d}x \\
  &= -\frac{x^2}{x \tan x + 1} + 2 \int \frac{x \cos x}{x \sin x + \cos x} \, \mathrm{d}x \\
  &= -\frac{x^2}{x \tan x + 1} + 2 \ln |x \sin x + \cos x| + C
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

</details>

### <details><summary>T2-分部积分</summary>

#### <details><summary>题意：</summary>

$$
I = \int \arcsin x \arccos x dx.
$$


</details>
<details> <summary><c>题解</c></summary>

熟悉下$\arccos x 和 \arcsin x$

$$
\begin{aligned}\\I &= x \arcsin x \arccos x - \int \frac{x(\arccos x - \arcsin x)}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \, dx \\\\&= x \arcsin x \arccos x + \int (\arccos x - \arcsin x) \, d\left(\sqrt{1-x^2}\right) \\\\&= x \arcsin x \arccos x + (\arccos x - \arcsin x)\sqrt{1-x^2} + 2 \int \, dx \\\\&= x \arcsin x \arccos x + (\arccos x - \arcsin x)\sqrt{1-x^2} + 2x + C.\\\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

</details>

### <details><summary>T3-分部积分-递推法</summary>

#### <details><summary>题意：</summary>

$$
求 I_{n}=\int \sin ^{n} x d x 的递推公式, 并利用所得结果求 \int \sin ^{4} x d x .
$$


</details>
<details> <summary><d>题解</d></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{n} &= \int \sin^{n-2} x (1 - \cos^2 x) \, dx \\
     &= \int \sin^{n-2} x \, dx - \int \sin^{n-2} x \cos^2 x \, dx \\
     &= I_{n-2} - \int \cos x \sin^{n-2} x \, d(\sin x) \\
     &= I_{n-2} - \frac{1}{n-1} \cos x \sin^{n-1} x - \frac{I_n}{n-1},
\end{aligned}
$$

故：

$$
I_{n}=\frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2}-\frac{1}{n}\cos x\sin^{n-1}x\ (n\neq0,1).
$$

由于$I_{0}=\int\mathrm{~d}x=x+C,$由上述结果得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{2} &= \frac{1}{2} I_{0} - \frac{1}{2} \cos x \sin x = \frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \sin 2x + \frac{C}{2}, \\
I_{4} &= \int \sin^{4} x \, dx = \frac{3}{4} I_{2} - \frac{1}{4} \cos x \sin^{3} x \\
     &= \frac{3}{4} \left( \frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \sin 2x + \frac{C}{2} \right) - \frac{1}{8} \sin 2x \sin^{2} x \\
     &= \frac{3}{8} x - \frac{1}{4} \sin 2x + \frac{1}{32} \sin 4x + C_{1} \quad \text{(其中 } C_{1} = \frac{3}{8} C).
\end{aligned}
$$

### <details><summary>T4</summary>

#### <details><summary>题意：</summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}& \text { 求 } I_{n}=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{x^{n} \sqrt{1+x^{2}}} \text { 的递推公式, 并利用所得结果求 } \int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{x^{3} \sqrt{1+x^{2}}}. \\& \end{aligned}
$$

</details>
<details> <summary><e>题解</e></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}\\I_{n} &= \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x^{2}+1\right)}{x^{n+1} \sqrt{1+x^{2}}} = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(\sqrt{x^{2}+1}\right)}{x^{n+1}} \\\\&= \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}{x^{n+1}} - \int \sqrt{x^{2}+1} \mathrm{~d}\left(\frac{1}{x^{n+1}}\right) \\\\&= \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}{x^{n+1}} + (n+1) \int \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}{x^{n+2}} \mathrm{~d} x \\\\&= \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}{x^{n+1}} + (n+1) \int \frac{x^{2}+1}{x^{n+2} \sqrt{x^{2}+1}} \mathrm{~d} x \\\\&= \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}}{x^{n+1}} + (n+1) I_{n} + (n+1) I_{n+2},\\\end{aligned}
$$

故：

$$
I_{n+2} = -\frac{n}{n+1}I_n - \frac{\sqrt{x^2+1}}{(n+1)x^{n+1}}.
$$

利用三角代换$x=\tan t,t\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{1} &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{1+x^{2}}} \\
     &= \int \frac{\sec^{2}t \, \mathrm{d}t}{\tan t \sec t} \\
     &= \int \csc t \, \mathrm{d}t \\
     &= \ln|\csc t - \cot t| + C \\
     &= \ln \frac{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}-1}{|x|} + C.
\end{aligned}
$$

由递推式：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{3} &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^{3} \sqrt{1 + x^{2}}} \\
     &= -\frac{1}{2} I_{1} - \frac{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}}}{2 x^{2}} \\
     &= -\frac{1}{2} \left( \ln \frac{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}} - 1}{|x|} + C \right) - \frac{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}}}{2 x^{2}} \\
     &= \frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}} + 1}{|x|} - \frac{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}}}{2 x^{2}} + C_{1} \quad \left( \text{其中 } C_{1} = -\frac{1}{2} C \right).
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

</details>
